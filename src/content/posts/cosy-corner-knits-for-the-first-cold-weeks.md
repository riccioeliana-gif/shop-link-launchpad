## Cosy corner: a few ideas

Just ideas, no rules:

- **Layer your softs.** A cardigan over a jumper over a tee. Take layers off as the day warms up.
- **One loud piece.** Let the fringe, the flowers or the stripes be the star, and keep the rest calm.
- **Or clash on purpose.** Pink with red, grey with fuchsia. Clashing is allowed. Encouraged, even.
- **Wrap up.** A long scarf can do the work of a whole outfit.
- **Make a corner.** Seriously: one cosy spot at home, a blanket, a mug, your favourite knit.
