Some days you don't want to rethink your whole outfit. You just want a little spark.

Good news: **small things do a lot of the talking**. A bag, an earring, a pouch that makes you smile every time you open it. Tiny pieces, big mood.

## Why the little stuff works

An accessory is the easiest way to play with colour. It goes with what you already own, it takes no time to add, and it can turn a plain tee and jeans into *your* look.

And if tomorrow you feel like something quieter? Swap it. No rules, no commitment.

## Pockets of joy: a few ideas

Just ideas, no rules:

- **Let one piece be loud.** A fuchsia bag with a calm outfit, or the other way round.
- **Keep it close.** A crossbody leaves your hands free and your things within reach.
- **Make the inside happy too.** A fluffy pouch for keys, lip balm and the little things you always need.
- **Small and shiny.** An earring with a heart or a sunburst catches the light and your eye.
- **Mix and match.** Pink with red, gold with fuchsia. Clashing is allowed. Encouraged, even.
