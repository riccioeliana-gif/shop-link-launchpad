This week, **16 to 20 November**, is **Anti-Bullying Week**. The theme for 2026 is **"Break the Silence"**.

The idea is simple: bullying grows where nobody talks about it. According to research cited by the Anti-Bullying Alliance, more than one in five children report being bullied every year. That's a lot of people carrying something heavy, often without saying a word.

## Why we care

Isar Things Shop is built on one promise: **everyone is welcome here, whoever they are**. No labels, no "his" and "hers", nobody who has to explain themselves. A happy place made of colours.

Feeling safe is part of that. And feeling safe often starts with one person who notices.

## Little ways to break the silence

No big speeches needed. Just a few ideas:

- **Notice.** The person eating alone, the friend who went quiet, the colleague who stopped joining in.
- **Sit next to them.** Sometimes that's the whole message: you're not alone.
- **Say a kind word.** Out loud, in a message, on a note. Kindness doesn't have to be loud to be heard.
- **Speak up.** If you see someone being picked on, say something, or tell someone you trust who can help.
- **Listen.** If someone tells you what's happening, believe them and stay with them.

## If it's happening to you

You didn't do anything to deserve it, and you don't have to deal with it alone. Talk to someone you trust, a friend, a teacher, a family member, or a local support service near you.
