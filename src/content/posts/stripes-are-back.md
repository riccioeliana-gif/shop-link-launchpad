If your feed looks like a candy shop lately, you're not imagining it: **stripes are back**. Not the polite navy-and-white kind. We're talking fuzzy, wobbly, slightly-too-many-colors stripes.

## Why now?

Honestly? Because everything else got so beige. After a few years of "quiet" wardrobes, people want a piece that says *hello, I'm here, I had fun getting dressed*. Stripes are the easiest way to do that, because they're loud but still make sense with almost anything.

## How to wear them

- **One stripe moment per outfit.** Let the sweater do the talking, keep the rest simple. Black jeans are your best friend.
- **Mix the scale.** A chunky stripe on top plays nicely with a thin stripe somewhere small, like a pouch or a sock.
- **Pick a color you already love.** Burgundy and pink go with more things than you'd expect.
