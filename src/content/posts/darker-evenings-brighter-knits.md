Heads up: this **Sunday, 25 October**, the clocks go back an hour. Hello extra sleep. Goodbye light at 6pm.

Shorter days can feel a bit grey. So here's our plan for the dark season: **turn the colour up**.

## Colour is the new daylight

When the sky goes beige, your jumper doesn't have to. A bright red, a soft pink, a juicy orange stripe: it's like carrying a little bit of summer around with you.

And no, you don't need a whole new wardrobe. One loud piece is enough to change the mood of a whole outfit.

## Little tricks for the long evenings

No rules, just a few ideas:

- **Start with one bright piece.** Then build the rest around it in calmer colours. Or don't. Clashing is cosy too.
- **Go for texture.** Fluffy, chunky, ribbed. The softer it feels, the warmer the evening.
- **Warm your head, brighten your face.** A coloured beanie does both at once.
- **Layer it up.** A cardigan over a tee works from the chilly morning to the warm café.
- **Keep the fun bits.** Hearts, bows, smileys. Dark evenings deserve a little silliness.
