Some days are just heavy. Too many messages, too many things to do, too little sleep.

**International Stress Awareness Week** runs from **4 to 8 November**, organised by the International Stress Management Association. So, a small note from us: you're allowed to slow down.

## Nobody has it all together

We're not experts, and clothes don't fix stress. But getting dressed shouldn't make a heavy day heavier. Sometimes the kindest thing is simply picking what feels soft.

## A few gentle ideas

No rules, just ideas for the days that need them:

- **Choose soft first.** Fluffy, fuzzy, teddy. Whatever feels kind on your skin.
- **Wear the easy thing.** The one you can throw on without thinking.
- **Keep something cosy close.** A little pouch with the things that calm you down: lip balm, a snack, headphones.
- **Take a breath.** Five slow ones count too.
- **Be a bit kinder to yourself.** You're doing more than you think.

## Be gentle with yourself

If today is a heavy one, you don't have to explain it. Take your time. Talk to someone you trust, or a local support service near you, whenever you need to.
