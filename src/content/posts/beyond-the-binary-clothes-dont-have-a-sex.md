A jumper doesn't know who's wearing it. A bag doesn't care. **Clothes don't have a sex.**

This Sunday, **8 November**, is the **Intersex Day of Solidarity**, also called the Intersex Day of Remembrance. It's marked by intersex organisations, groups and allies, and it began in 2005.

## Why the day exists

The day honours Herculine Barbin, a French intersex person whose memoirs were published long after their death. It's a day to stand with intersex people and to talk about a world that too often expects everyone to fit into just two boxes.

We're not experts, and this isn't our story to tell. If you want to learn more, listen to intersex people and their organisations first.

## What we can say for sure

At Isar Things Shop there are no "his" and "hers" sections. No boxes. Every piece is simply a piece, picked by hand, and anyone can wear it.

That's it. That's the whole idea:

- **No labels on the hanger.** A top is a top.
- **Wear it how you like.** Oversized, tucked, layered, knotted.
- **Skip the aisle.** Start from the colour, the texture, the mood.
- **Ask yourself one thing.** Do you feel good in it?
