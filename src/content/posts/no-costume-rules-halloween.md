Halloween is tomorrow, **Saturday 31 October**, and you don't need a costume aisle to join in.

Here's our rule for tonight: **no "for him", no "for her"**. A costume is whoever you want to be for one evening. A cat, a sailor, a ghost, a tomato. Your call.

## Start with what you already own

Most good costumes are 90% regular clothes and one funny detail. Open your wardrobe and look for:

- **Stripes.** Striped tops are secretly a hundred costumes: sailor, pirate, mime, painter.
- **One bold colour.** Head to toe in a single shade is instant character.
- **Things with ears, hearts or faces.** The silly details do the work for you.
- **Whatever you'd wear anyway.** A costume you feel comfortable in is a costume you'll enjoy.
