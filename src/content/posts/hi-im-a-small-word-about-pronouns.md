"Hi, I'm Sam, I use they/them." Short sentence. Big welcome.

Tomorrow, **Wednesday 21 October**, is **International Pronouns Day**.

## What the day is about

International Pronouns Day started in 2018 as a grassroots project, and it happens every year on the third Wednesday of October. There's no big organisation behind it: people, schools and groups everywhere just make it their own.

The idea is simple. Make it normal to share your pronouns, ask for other people's, and respect them. Because using the words someone chose for themselves is a basic part of treating them with dignity.

## Why it feels like home to us

If you've been here before, you know our first rule: **no "his" and "hers"**. A cardigan is a cardigan. A top is a top. Whoever wears it decides what it means.

Pronouns work the same way. Nobody else gets to label you. You say who you are, and the people around you listen. That's it. That's the whole thing.

## Small ways to join in

No rules, just a few easy ideas:

- **Introduce yourself with yours.** "I'm Alex, she/her." It makes it easier for everyone else to do the same.
- **Ask, don't guess.** "What pronouns do you use?" is a kind question, not an awkward one.
- **Got it wrong? Fix it and move on.** A quick "sorry, they" is better than a big fuss.
- **Add them where you like.** Your bio, your email signature, your name badge. Only if and when you want to.
- **Remember it's personal.** Some people share, some don't. Both are fine.
